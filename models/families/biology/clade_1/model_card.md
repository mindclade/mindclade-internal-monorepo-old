# Model card: Clade 1

- **Status:** Scaffold template; no model release is represented by this file.
- **Family:** `clade_1`

## Intended use

Describe approved biological/modeling capabilities, target users, deployment
modes, and explicitly prohibited or restricted uses.

## Required release record

A promoted model card must identify architecture/config/weight/runtime bundle
digests, training and evaluation data versions, objective and optimization
summary, hardware/toolchain, checkpoint lineage, supported inputs/outputs,
precision and kernel policy, performance, calibration, robustness, privacy,
safety and biological-risk evaluations, known limitations, access controls,
and rollback/revocation procedure.

## Evidence

Claims must link immutable numerical, capability, safety, security, scale, and
serving qualification evidence. Model weights remain protected artifacts and
may be loaded only through audited grants.

This scaffold does not claim accuracy, safety, readiness, or availability.
