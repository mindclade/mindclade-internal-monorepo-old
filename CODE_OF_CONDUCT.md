# Code of conduct

Mindclade engineering work must be professional, respectful, evidence-driven,
and safe. Contributors are expected to:

- critique designs and code without attacking people;
- disclose uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and material risks;
- protect confidential company, customer, partner, model, and dataset data;
- respect scientific integrity, licensing, consent, privacy, and safety review;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, retaliation, or intimidation;
- escalate security and biological-risk concerns promptly and in good faith.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict access, or suspend participation when
behavior threatens people, security, research integrity, or the reliability of
the platform. Concerns should be raised privately with engineering leadership
or the security/safety owner when public discussion would expose sensitive
information.
