# Experiment hypothesis

- **Status:** Example scaffold; not an approved experiment.

## Hypothesis

State one falsifiable claim and the mechanism expected to produce the result.

## Experimental design

Record baseline, treatment, controlled variables, dataset/model/config digests,
seeds, hardware, metrics, sample size, stopping rule, and statistical test.

## Risks and integrity

Describe leakage, confounding, safety, privacy, licensing, and hidden-evaluation
risks. Pre-register any decision threshold that could otherwise be selected
after seeing the result.

## Outcome

Record evidence, uncertainty, negative results, and whether the hypothesis was
supported. Research output may consume production packages but must not become
a production dependency without review and qualification.
